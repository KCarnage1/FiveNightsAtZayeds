# Five Nights at Zayed's: the lore (spoilers)

This is the full story behind FNAZ 1 and FNAZ 2. **Established** means it was already in FNAZ 1. **New** means added for FNAZ 2. Everything marked "to confirm" about Ibrahim, Gelly and Bidoof is a proposal until you send their pictures and say what you want.

## Established (FNAZ 1)
- You play **Rashid**, the night guard.
- **Abdulla's Cafeteria** had one phone line for delivery orders. When the cafeteria closed, the number went to **Salem's Candy**. Same line, same ring. That is why the **Falcon delivers**, and why **the gold camel (Abdulla) picks up the phone** whenever anyone calls anyone.
- The **Zayeds are one soul in four bodies** because it "didn't fit, so it split": the Camel is the **patience**, the Cat is the **nerve**, the Goat is the **memory**, the Falcon is the **hunger**.
- The phone guy works at **Qerh Cooperations**, handled the cafeteria's file, read Rashid's police report and **reopened it**. The police arrive at six. The Falcon, on skates, ends the phone guy's call with "Special delivery."
- A note about a **cupcake**: "Do not eat it."

## New: what really happened (the answer to the puzzle)
**Order #0.** In the week the cafeteria closed, its number was moved to the Salem's Candy counter. That evening someone rang the old number and ordered **twelve cupcakes, four candles on each, delivered by six**. **Rashid, seventeen and behind the counter, wrote it down and went on his break.** The order never reached the kitchen. **Abdulla**, still closing up the kitchen, heard the extension, picked it up and promised it would arrive. It never did. The next morning the police found the building empty, the kitchen warm and the phone off the hook. That is "Order Night".

An order that can be neither cancelled nor completed **split into four things it needs to stay alive**: someone to **wait** (Camel, patience), someone to **call** (Cat, nerve), someone to **remember** (Goat, memory) and someone to **deliver** (Falcon, hunger). That is the "one soul, four bodies." The soul is **an unfinished delivery**, and it is anchored to the person who wrote the order down. They are not coming to hurt Rashid. They are coming to deliver what he took.

## New: the rest of the cast (to confirm)
- **Ibrahim (music box).** The caller **on hold**. The music box tune is the old line's **hold music**; while it plays, he is still on the line. If it stops, the line drops, and he comes through to find out why. He has been holding since the night of the order.
- **Gelly (vents).** A **Salem's Candy mascot** built to ride the cafeteria's **order chute**, the kitchen-to-tables dumbwaiter that runs behind the party rooms and out through the ceiling vents. It was built to deliver and still delivers. It just never knocks.
- **Bidoof (the laugher).** The **Complaints Department bell**. It rings once per complaint, never hurts anyone, and takes your flashlight, because light means the shop is open and the shop is closed.
- **Golden Abdulla.** The owner who stayed behind. He picks up the phone.

## New: why the FNAZ 2 rules exist
- **No doors:** Qerh's slogan for the new location was "open door policy": the order must always be able to arrive.
- **Toy bodies:** Qerh's "Project Fresh Start" moved the soul out of the broken Withered bodies into new Toy bodies. It split into four every time. The Falcon keeps going back to its old body.
- **The Zayed mask:** the old cafeteria uniform cap. Wearing it marks you as staff, on the clock, so most of the delivery bots leave you alone. The Withered Falcon (hungry) and Ibrahim (holding) do not care.
- **The flashlight:** Qerh-issue safety light. **The vents:** the old order chute.

## How players find it out (nothing is explained outright)
**Case file (Extras).** Twelve papers are hidden in the cameras and your office, appearing from the night they were filed. Click one to pick it up (it replaces the flashlight click). Each night's phone call is filed as a transcript after you survive it.

| Paper | Night | Where | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Menu board | 1 | Main Hall | One line, never busy; "ask for the Falcon" |
| Newspaper clipping | 1 | Your office | The closing, the empty building, the phone off the hook |
| Order slip No. 4 | 2 | Party Room 1 | Camel: patience |
| Order slip No. 7 | 2 | Left vent | Cat: nerve |
| Order slip No. 1 | 3 | Prize Corner (on the music box) | Goat: memory, "12 cupcakes, four candles" |
| Transfer log | 3 | Parts & Service whiteboard | Qerh's soul transfers; it splits in four |
| Card under the music box | 3 | Prize Corner | The tune is hold music; Ibrahim |
| Order slip No. 2 | 4 | Game Area | Falcon: hunger; "deliver by six" |
| Order chute blueprint | 4 | Kid's Cove | The vents and Gelly |
| Tag on Bidoof | 4 | The stage | Complaints bell |
| Police report | 5 | Parts & Service | The witness "R.", age 17, took the order |
| Cupcake with a note | 6 | Your desk | In Rashid's own handwriting |

**The puzzle.** The four order slips have the numbers **4, 7, 1, 2**. The final call tells you to "put them in the order the bodies were made": Camel, Cat, Goat, Falcon, so **4712**. Dialling it in the Case file unlocks the last page, **Order #0**, the answer above.

**The phone calls** each add a clue: papers taped around the building; the vents are the old order chute; the music box is hold music; keep the slip numbers; the witness's initial is "on every slip."
