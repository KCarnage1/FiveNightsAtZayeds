# Voice script: Five Nights at Zayed's 2

For voice actors. Every line is in the game's lore (see `lore-bible.md`). Record each line a few times, clean and with a "worse" take, so there is something to choose from. File names follow the line numbers (for example `phone_1_3.wav`, `toyzayed_office_2.wav`).

**General notes**
- The animatronics are one soul split in four. The same four traits run through every body: the Camel waits, the Cat dares, the Goat remembers, the Falcon is hungry.
- **Toy** voices are bright, polished, a little too cheerful, like a recorded greeting. **Withered** voices are the same person after thirty years: slower, lower, with gaps where the audio skips.
- Nobody is evil. They are trying to deliver an order. The scariest reading is polite and patient, not angry.
- Keep lines short. Most play for two to three seconds over a game sound.
- Lines marked *(whisper)* are close-mic. Lines marked *(effect)* are recorded clean and processed later.

---

## Part 1: The phone guy, nights 1 to 5
Qerh Cooperations claims adjuster. Mid-thirties to fifties, friendly, a little nervous, always trying to sound like this is a normal job. He calls from his desk at night. Record every call as one continuous take, then split. Add phone-line filter (effect).

### Night 1
*Direction:* Warm, upbeat, a little rehearsed, like a man reading a welcome script he has read many times. Smile in the voice. Slow down on "Do not go looking for them."

1.1  Hello? Hello, yoo-hoo! Welcome to your first night at the new Zayed's, Rashid. I'm the phone guy from Qerh Cooperations.

1.2  You may have heard about the old place. Forget it. This is the new location. Brand new bodies. Shiny. Friendly.

1.3  We took the doors away. Insurance reasons, and also a lot of broken hinges. Use your flashlight down the hall, and if anything walks in, put the Zayed mask on. They think you are one of them.

1.4  The new bodies are called the Toys. The old ones are in Parts and Service. Do not go looking for them.

1.5  One more thing from Qerh Cooperations. There is old paperwork taped up around the building, left over from before. Do not read it. If you do read it, do not click on anything you find on the cameras.

### Night 2
*Direction:* Slightly tired, trying to stay cheerful. A breath before "Some orders are still coming up." Lower the voice, like he is looking over his shoulder.

2.1  Hello! Night two. You made it. I'm honestly relieved, the paperwork was getting heavy.

2.2  Two air vents open into your office, left and right. Something small and fast will use them. Mask on when it arrives, and it will leave.

2.3  Those vents are older than the building. They were the order chute at the old cafeteria. Food went down, orders came up. Some orders are still coming up.

2.4  The old bodies are awake now too. The Withered ones. The Falcon is the worst of them. He runs the hallway, and no mask will stop him. Light. Down the hall. Remember that.

### Night 3
*Direction:* Quieter, closer to the mic. Gentle and sad on the hold-music line. Leave a long pause before "Do not let it stop."

3.1  Hi, Rashid. A few things before you start.

3.2  There is a music box in the Prize Corner. Wind it from the camera, hold the button, and keep it wound. It is not a toy. It belongs to someone named Ibrahim.

3.3  Ibrahim is not one of the four. The Zayeds are one soul, split into four bodies: the Camel is the patience, the Cat is the nerve, the Goat is the memory, the Falcon is the hunger. Ibrahim is... somebody else. Nobody at Qerh Cooperations will say who.

3.4  If the music stops, he comes out. Do not let it stop.

3.5  The tune is a recording from the old phone line. Hold music. I will say no more, because I think you can work out what it means to be on hold for thirty years.

### Night 4
*Direction:* Hushed and hurried, because he is not supposed to be saying any of this. Speed up on the two things he was asked not to tell you. Brighten for one beat on "a little round one that laughs", then go flat again.

4.1  Hello. Night four. Two things I was asked not to tell you.

4.2  One: something new is crawling in the ceiling. Her name is Gelly. She uses the vents like a hallway. Mask works on her, but she will not leave quickly.

4.3  Two: there is a little round one that laughs. Bidoof. He does not hurt anyone. He just breaks your flashlight, and that is worse. Put the mask on and he goes away.

4.4  By the way, the old Abdulla's Cafeteria phone line still rings in this building. Same line as Salem's Candy. That is why the Falcon delivers. I hope you never hear it ring.

4.5  If you find slips of paper on the cameras, order slips, each with a number, do not throw them away. Claims adjusters keep everything. Write down the numbers. Numbers have an order, and orders have a reason.

### Night 5
*Direction:* Fast, low, nervous. Almost whispering. Stumble once on "I am being watched". Sound like he is reading from a file he should not have, and drop to a flat voice for the police report line.

5.1  Rashid. Almost there. I will be quick, I am being watched.

5.2  I read the old police report. The cafeteria's file. Your name is in it. At Qerh Cooperations I am the one who handles files, and tonight I reopened yours.

5.3  The soul that was split did not want to be split. Four bodies is a lot of anger to carry. Do not give it a reason.

5.4  Last night is tomorrow. Whatever you hear at the door at six, do not open it for anyone.

5.5  The report names a witness who was seventeen at the time, behind the counter at Salem's Candy. I will not say the initial. You know it. They put it on every slip.



**Optional pickups** (short, for in-game reactions): "Hello? Hello?"  /  "Are you still there?"  /  "Yoo-hoo!"  /  (a long pause, then) "Ah. Good luck."

## Part 2: The animatronics
Each character has: a voice, an **idle** line (heard through the cameras now and then), an **arrival** line (they are at your vent or in the hall), an **in the office** line (just before they strike, if you are not masked), a **leaves** line (when you put the mask on), a **jumpscare** sound (non-speech), and one **secret** line for the case file.

### Toy Zayed (Camel, patience)
Voice: a friendly waiter. Warm, unhurried, never raises his voice. Think restaurant host.
- idle: "Your table will be ready shortly."
- idle: "No rush. We can wait."
- arrival: "Evening, Rashid. Just checking in."
- in the office: "Your order is almost ready."
- leaves *(when masked)*: "Oh. Pardon me, I thought you were someone else."
- jumpscare: a long, low "Special delivery" held on one note, then a hard cut. (effect)
- secret: "I have been waiting since six. I do not mind."

### Toy Bubbles (Cat, nerve)
Voice: bold, cheeky, always on the edge of a giggle. Fast. Likes the sound of her own voice.
- idle: "Ring ring! Is this the right number?"
- idle *(giggle)*: "Hee. I made the call, you know."
- arrival *(from the vent)*: "Knock knock! Oh. I forgot. I do not knock."
- in the office: "Do not hang up. Do not hang up. Do not hang up."
- leaves *(when masked)*: "Wrong number. Sorry!"
- jumpscare: a sharp cat hiss that turns into a dial tone. (effect)
- secret: "I was the one who rang. Somebody had to say it out loud."

### Toy Taffy (Goat, memory)
Voice: soft, dreamy, sing-song. Repeats details like a nursery rhyme.
- idle: "Twelve cupcakes. Four candles each."
- idle: "Do not forget the candles."
- arrival: "I remember you, Rashid. You had a pencil."
- in the office: "Twelve cupcakes. Four candles each. Twelve cupcakes. Four candles each."
- leaves *(when masked)*: "Mm. That was not you. I remember."
- jumpscare: the same number counted down, faster and faster, ending in silence. (effect)
- secret: "I remember the order. I do not remember who took it. Do you?"

### Toy Sherbet (Falcon, hunger)
Voice: fast, breathless, excitable, hungry. A delivery rider who has been running all night. Roller-skate noise under the lines.
- idle: "Skates, skates, skates."
- idle: "Is it ready? Is it ready yet?"
- arrival: "Special delivery!"
- in the office: "I am so hungry. I am so late."
- leaves *(when masked)*: "Not you. Not yet. Next one."
- jumpscare: a high shriek on the word "DELIVERY", cut off by the sound of skates. (effect)
- secret: "I deliver it or I disappear. That is the whole deal."

### Withered Zayed
Voice: the same waiter, old and cracked. Slow. A tape that is wearing out.
- idle *(skipping)*: "Your... table will be... shortly."
- arrival: "Rashid. We kept your place."
- in the office: "We waited. We are still waiting."
- leaves: "...Pardon... me..."
- jumpscare: a slowed-down, pitched-down "delivery." (effect)

### Withered Bubbles
Voice: the same cheeky voice, but broken into pieces and static. The giggle has turned into a rattle.
- idle: "Ring... ring... ring."
- arrival *(from the vent)*: "Is anyone... there?"
- in the office: "Pick up. Pick up. Pick up."
- leaves: "...wrong... number..."
- jumpscare: a rattling laugh cut by a busy signal. (effect)

### Withered Taffy
Voice: a gentle voice, now flat and endlessly patient, like someone who has told the story too many times.
- idle: "Twelve... four... twelve... four..."
- arrival: "I kept the candles."
- in the office: "Do you remember? You were supposed to remember."
- leaves: "Ah. Wrong face."
- jumpscare: the numbers counted in a flat monotone, then a single loud bell. (effect)

### Withered Sherbet (runs the hall)
Voice: a ragged, hoarse, exhausted rider who has been running for thirty years. Gasping.
- hall alert *(the moment he starts to run)*: "DELIVERY!"
- hall, while running: "Faster, faster, faster."
- pushed back by the flashlight: "Too bright. Not yet. Not yet."
- jumpscare: a full-throated scream of "SPECIAL DELIVERY," then skates. (effect)

### Ibrahim (the music box, "on hold")
Voice: a man on an old phone line, muffled and tinny, politely patient. Hums along to the music box. **Record everything through a telephone filter.** Never raises his voice until the end.
- idle *(while the box is wound)*: a soft hum along to the tune.
- idle: "Still holding."
- box running low: "Hello? Is anyone there?"
- box empty, the moment it stops: "...Hello? ...Hello? ...I think we were cut off."
- coming out: "Thank you for holding."
- jumpscare: "YOUR CALL IS IMPORTANT TO US," torn apart by static. (effect)
- secret: "I only asked for a table, a cake and some candles. I have been on hold for thirty years."

### Gelly (the vents, the order chute)
Voice: a whispery, giggling mascot. Child-like, playful, a little unsettling. Always slightly closer than it should be.
- idle *(in the vents)*: "Down the chute... down the chute..."
- arrival: "Knock knock. Oh, never mind."
- in the office: "Delivery for you! I never knock."
- leaves *(when masked)*: "Aww. Wrong room."
- jumpscare: a gleeful squeal, then a clatter like something dropped down a metal chute. (effect)
- secret: "I was built to hand things out. Nobody takes them."

### Bidoof (the complaints bell)
Voice: a pure, round, delighted laugh. Very few words. The laugh is the whole performance.
- idle: "Hee! Hee hee!"
- arrival: "Complaint received!"
- in the office *(flashlight dies)*: a rolling laugh, then a single clear bell ring. (effect)
- leaves *(when masked)*: "Aww."
- secret: "Closed! Closed! Closed!"

### Golden Abdulla (the owner, the phone)
Voice: an old man, warm and gravelly, the sort of owner who knows every customer by name. This is the most dangerous voice because it is the kindest.
- phone pickup: "Abdulla's Cafeteria. Abdulla speaking."
- promise: "Do not worry. I will see to it myself."
- on the stage, in the dark: "Your order is ready."
- the final line, only when the player has solved the case file: "It was never your fault, Rashid. It was only never finished."

## Part 3: Line IDs, triggers and durations (for the sound team)
| Character | Trigger in the game | Count | Target length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone guy | Start of nights 1 to 5 | 5 calls (22 lines) | 20 to 55 s per call |
| Each Toy / Withered body | idle, arrival, in the office, leaves, jumpscare | 5 per body | 1.5 to 4 s |
| Ibrahim | box wound, box low, box empty, coming out, jumpscare | 6 | 1 to 4 s |
| Gelly, Bidoof | idle, arrival, in the office, leaves, jumpscare | 5 each | 1 to 3 s |
| Golden Abdulla | phone, promise, stage, final | 4 | 2 to 5 s |
| Secret lines | unlocked by the case file | 1 per character | 3 to 6 s |

The game currently shows the phone calls as subtitles read by the device's text-to-speech. Recorded files will replace that automatically once they are added.
